How to permanently remove your JosRide data

You can delete your JosRide rider or driver account from the website or the app. Deletion is permanent. Active trips must be finished first.

On the website

  1. Sign in to the rider or driver portal.
  2. Open Settings, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm the warning. You will be signed out when the request is submitted.
Sign in as rider Sign in as driver

In the app

Open Profile or Security & privacy, then choose Delete account and confirm twice.

What we delete

  • Account profile, phone, and login access
  • Trip history, scheduled rides, and in-app messages
  • Wallet records associated with the account
  • Saved locations and trusted contacts

Payment processors such as Paystack may retain transaction records required by law. We may keep limited records needed for fraud, safety, or legal obligations.
